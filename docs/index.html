What we do

What we treat.

Not sure what you need? Come in, show us, and we’ll tell you.

  1. 01

    Check-ups

    A proper look at your teeth and gums, and honest advice on what, if anything, needs doing.

  2. 02

    Cleaning

    Scaling and polishing for the tartar a toothbrush can’t shift.

  3. 03

    Fillings

    Tooth-coloured fillings for cavities and small chips.

  4. 04

    Tooth pain

    Come in when it hurts. We find out why before we talk about treatment.

  5. 05

    Root canal

    To save an infected tooth instead of losing it.

  6. 06

    Extractions

    Including wisdom teeth, when there’s no better option.

  7. 07

    Children’s teeth

    Unhurried first visits, so the dentist never becomes something to dread.

  8. 08

    Dentures

    Full and partial, adjusted until they sit right.

Questions

Questions we get a lot.

Do I need an appointment?

It helps. Book online or give us a call and we’ll tell you when’s quietest. If you just turn up, we’ll do our best to fit you in.

Are you really open every day?

Yes, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., including Sundays. Festival days can be different, so call first around holidays.

How much will it cost?

It depends on what you need. We’ll tell you the cost before starting anything, so there are no surprises.

Can I bring my child?

Of course. Children are welcome; please come with them for the visit.

What if it’s an emergency at night?

Outside our hours, please go to your nearest hospital. During the day, call us straight away.
